Privacy

Local-first history, with Apple system features when you choose them.

Sunclub has no app-owned account system, no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking. Optional private iCloud sync, notifications, location, HealthKit, and backup export are controlled by you.

  • No app-owned accounts.
  • No ads or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Optional private iCloud sync and local backup export.
  • Optional notifications, location features, and HealthKit.
  • Live UV uses Apple Weather only when you turn it on.
  • Read the privacy policy.
