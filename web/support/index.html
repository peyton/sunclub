Common questions

How do I correct an application?

On Today, tap Edit log. For another day, open History and select the date. You can correct application times, SPF, coverage, and notes.

Why didn’t a reminder appear?

Check Sunclub’s notification permission and Focus settings in iOS, then Settings → Reminders in Sunclub. Open the app periodically to keep reminders up to date. Logging and reapplication tracking work with notifications off.

What is a leave-home check-in?

With Home saved and background location allowed, Sunclub can ask whether you applied when you leave. Choose Already applied and confirm the time, snooze, or dismiss. An unanswered check-in does not count as an application.

Why does a widget look out of date?

Open Sunclub, then return to the widget. Widgets and Apple Watch show application timing and let you log or reapply directly.

Can I use Sunclub without location?

Yes. Logging and scheduled reminders work without it. Choose a city for Apple Weather forecasts, or use the labeled local UV estimate. About UV data.

How do backups and iCloud work?

Manage private iCloud sync and backup import/export in Settings. Imports restore locally first. Review them before choosing to publish to iCloud. Recovery & Changes contains available undo and restore options.

Where are automation permissions?

Open Settings → Shortcuts to manage Shortcut writes, link opens, link writes, and result details. See the Shortcuts guide.

For troubleshooting, include your device, iOS version, and what happened.
