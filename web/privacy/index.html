Privacy Details

Sunclub is built as a local-first sunscreen habit tracker. You do not need an account, we do not show ads, and your history is not sold.

Your data stays yours. No ads. No app account. You stay in control.

Your data stays yours

Sunclub stores sunscreen logs, optional SPF details, notes, reminders, widgets, backup state, and history needed to run the features you choose.

No accounts or ads

Sunclub does not run app-owned accounts, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

iCloud is optional

If you use iCloud sync, your history syncs through your private iCloud database so it can follow you across Apple devices.

You stay in control

You can export your history, import a backup, delete local app data by removing the app, and manage synced data in Apple settings.

Location and UV

Location is optional. When you allow it, Sunclub can show local UV context and forecast guidance. Manual logging works without location.

Notifications

Sunclub uses local notifications only after you allow them. Reminders can help you apply, reapply, or review your week.

Apple Shortcuts

Shortcuts can log sunscreen or check status using your Sunclub settings. You can turn automation writes off in the app.

Camera, HealthKit, and backups

Camera, HealthKit, backup export, and backup import are used only when you choose those features. Apple system permissions can be changed later.

Privacy questions: privacy@mail.sunclub.peyton.app. Support: support@mail.sunclub.peyton.app. Security reports: security@mail.sunclub.peyton.app. General questions: contact@mail.sunclub.peyton.app.

Medical note

Sunclub is a habit tracker, not a medical device or medical advice. Sunscreen needs vary by skin, product, activity, weather, location, and medical history. Follow product directions and consult a qualified professional for medical questions.

Changes
