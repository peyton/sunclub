Getting Started

Start with optional reminders, then log sunscreen on Today. Choose other preferences whenever you need them in Settings.

1

Turn on reminders

Tap Get Started, then enable reminders or choose Not now. Both choices lead to Today after setup is saved. Change daily, reapply and leave-home reminders later in Settings, then Reminders.

2

Log your sunscreen

Tap Log sunscreen on Today after you apply. Use Edit to add optional SPF, coverage or notes. Log reapplication stays available when reminders are off. Save your sunscreen profile in Settings.

3

Choose your UV settings

In Settings, open UV & Weather to use your current location or choose a city. Location is optional; cached and estimated UV remain labeled with their source. Apple Health has its own optional Settings page.

4

Check your progress

Use the Today, History, and Settings tabs to move around. Open Insights from History for the last seven days and streak. Pick dates and edit logs in History; Today stays on the current day. Apple Watch, widgets and Shortcuts keep logging close by.

Tip: consistency is key

Follow your sunscreen label, including after sweating or swimming, and follow the directions on your sunscreen.
