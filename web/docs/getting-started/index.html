Getting Started

Welcome to Sunclub. Follow these steps to build your sunscreen habit and keep your next action easy to find.

1

Choose your UV settings

Sunclub can use your location to show local UV Index and forecast context. You can allow location during setup, change it later in Settings, or keep manual logging without location.

2

Log your sunscreen

Tap Log Today when you apply sunscreen. Add SPF, product, areas, or notes when they help, or keep the log quick when you are on the go.

3

Turn on reminders

Daily and reapply reminders are optional. When reminders are on, Sunclub can nudge you when it is time to apply again.

4

Check your progress

Use Today for your current status and History for the bigger pattern. Apple Watch, widgets, and Shortcuts can keep the same habit close by.

Tip: consistency is key

Reapply every two hours, especially after sweating or swimming, and follow the directions on your sunscreen.
